Space Center Houston: As the official visitor center of NASA Johnson Space Center, it allows visitors to explore space capsules, moon rocks, and astronaut training facilities. Big Bend National Park: Located in West Texas, this vast park encompasses the entire Chisos mountain range and large swaths of the Chihuahuan Desert, offering dramatic canyons along the Rio Grande. The State Capitol in Austin: Completed in 1888, the Texas State Capitol building is made of sunset red granite and stands taller than the U.S. Capitol in Washington, D.C.
